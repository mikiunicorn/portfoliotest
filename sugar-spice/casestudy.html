THE BAKER'S PERSPECTIVE

My Story

"Before becoming a product designer, I spent 8 years in professional kitchens as a pastry chef. Working in high-end restaurants and gift-oriented bakeries, I witnessed the 'magic' of a surprise cake — but also the deep friction behind the scenes. I saw customers struggle with complex ordering, last-minute logistics, and the stress of transporting fragile products."

This project is the culmination of my culinary expertise and my design vision — solving a real-world problem I lived every day in the kitchen.

THE PROBLEM

A Logistical Nightmare

Through industry experience and research, three core pain points emerged for both senders and recipients.

🍞
Preparation Burden

The time and effort required for baking, ingredient shopping, and cleanup creates a high barrier to entry for spontaneous gifting.

EFFORT & TIME
🚗
Logistical Friction

High anxiety surrounding the safe transport and delivery of delicate cakes. Fragile products demand careful handling that most people can't guarantee.

DELIVERY STRESS
📅
Planning Fatigue

Traditional artisanal orders often require planning far in advance, which doesn't suit the spontaneous, emotional nature of gift-giving.

CURRENT VERSION
CONCLUSIONS & LEARNING

Key Takeaways

01 📊
Data-Driven Simplification

The research shifted the focus from "full customization" to "smart templates," which significantly improved user flow and reduced decision fatigue.

02 🎨
Brand Boldness

Moving away from traditional "soft" bakery aesthetics to a high-contrast pixel style created a more memorable and unique brand identity that stands out.

03 🤖
Technological Synergy

Integrating AI into the design and development workflow allowed for faster prototyping and a more robust responsive system with consistent design language.
